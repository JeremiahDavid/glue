## How pricing works

You buy **one Mesh** — a connected set of systems (typically ops + QuickBooks + Excel).

**Flat Mesh price** includes **all catalog Signals** that apply to that Mesh. There is **no per-Signal fee**.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mesh** | The systems you connect (e.g. ServiceTitan + QuickBooks Online + Excel) |
| **Signal** | One catalog exception / insight pack (e.g. closed work not invoiced) |
| **Applicable** | A Signal is included when every required system role on its catalog card is present on your Mesh |

**Commercial sequence:** We discover your systems and **implement** the Mesh and applicable Signals at **$0**, then you run a **free 2-week trial**. Activation and monthly fees apply **only if you convert** to a paid subscription.

```
Discovery + Mesh build ($0)  →  2-week trial ($0)  →  Convert?  →  $4,000 activation + $600/mo
                                                      └─ Walk away — no fee
```

## FAQ

**Do I pay for implementation?**  
No. Standard Mesh implementation for the trial is **free**. You pay activation only if you convert after the trial.

**Do I get unlimited Signals?**  
You get **all catalog Signals that apply to your Mesh** — not every Signal in the universe, and not custom metrics.

**What if you add a new catalog Signal later that fits my Mesh?**  
New applicable catalog Signals for your Mesh are **included** at no extra activation as the product matures.

**Can I buy just one Signal?**  
No. Meshflow is sold as a **Mesh**. Signals are the outcomes of that Mesh, not à la carte SKUs.

**What if I need a fourth system or a second stack?**  
See add-ons (M4 or second Mesh).
